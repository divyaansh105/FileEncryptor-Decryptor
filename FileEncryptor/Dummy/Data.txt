Waterfalls are one of nature's most breathtaking displays, formed when a river or stream flows over a vertical drop or a series of steep 
drops in the landscape. They can vary in size from small cascades to towering falls that plunge hundreds of feet. Waterfalls often occur 
in the upper course of a river where the flow of water has enough energy to erode the rock beneath it, creating dramatic scenery. These 
natural wonders are not only beautiful but also play a role in shaping the environment by influencing erosion patterns and supporting 
diverse ecosystems. Many waterfalls have become popular tourist destinations, admired for their power, beauty, and the soothing sound 
of rushing water.
